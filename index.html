Frequently asked

Important rental notes.

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Pets are allowed with an added pet deposit.

Do I need a special driver's license?

No. A standard Class C driver's license is enough for these rentals, though large vehicle experience is required for the larger rigs.

Can I tow a car or boat?

Yes, up to 5,000 lbs with a tow fee and security deposit.

Can I do a one-way rental?

No. The RV Krew currently does not offer one-way rental options.

Can I travel outside California?

Most rentals are statewide. Trips outside California require written authorization by contract, and special events or festivals require prior approval.

Are levelers provided?

Motorhomes include either automatic leveling systems or drive-on leveling pads.

Are there charges besides the rental cost?

Rental agreements can vary, so review your contract for fees. A cleaning fee is required, while most other charges are optional or situational.

Can I park my car while I travel?

Public street parking may be available near the facility, but The RV Krew is not responsible for personal vehicles. Ride sharing is recommended for pickup.

Can I store items outside the motorhome?

Motorhomes have outdoor storage compartments, and hitch rack options may be available for an added fee.

Do the motorhomes have awnings or sun shades?

Yes. Most rigs have manual or automatic rollout awnings.

Do you provide baby car seats or cribs?

No. Child seats are not provided, but RVs include anchor points so you can install your own child seat.

Does the motorhome come equipped with pots and pans?

Kitchen amenities vary by unit. Bringing your own kitchen items is recommended.

Does the motorhome come with a TV?

Yes. Rental units come equipped with flatscreen Smart TVs.

How do I learn to operate the motorhome?

At pickup, a manager provides a live walkthrough of the RV and its main functions.

How many people can ride in the motorhome?

Only the people listed on the reservation may ride in the motorhome, based on the available seats and seatbelts.

How much do campgrounds cost?

Campground costs vary by location and by the amenities provided.

Is insurance coverage provided with the rental?

Insurance coverage is the renter's responsibility through a personal auto policy or an approved third-party rental insurance provider.

Is the customer responsible for maintenance?

No. Motorhomes are fleet maintained and inspected before and after each rental.

Is there a charge for generator use?

Some generator use is included. Excessive use may incur hourly charges based on the RV's generator meter.

Can renters access the roof?

No. Some units have ladders, but renters are not allowed to access the roof. Ladders are for management use only.

Where can I buy supplies, propane, or dump holding tanks nearby?

Western Propane in Garden Grove is recommended for propane refills and dumping. A Walmart within a few miles of the rental facility can cover most other supplies.

What counts as a rental day?

A rental day is 24 hours after pickup time and each 24-hour period after that until the vehicle is returned.

What happens if the vehicle I reserved is unavailable?

The RV Krew will try to place you in another suitable unit. If that is not possible, fees paid will be refunded or credited toward a future rental.

What if I need to cancel my reservation?

Email management at TheRvKrew@gmail.com. Cancellations made within 72 hours of pickup are not refunded.

What if the motorhome breaks down?

Rigs are maintained and inspected before each rental. If something happens on the road, roadside assistance packages are available.

What is the cost of mileage?

Rentals include 100 miles per rental day. Additional miles are charged at return.

What kind of motorhomes do you rent?

The fleet includes multiple classes and sizes of motorhomes, with inventory expanding over time. Contact The RV Krew for current availability.

What security deposits are required?

Security deposits vary based on the model, rental terms, insurance deductible, and selected add-ons.

What time are pickup and drop-off?

Standard pickup and drop-off are at 11 AM. Times may be flexible with prior arrangement.
