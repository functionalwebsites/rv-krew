Frequently asked

Important rental notes.

Are pets allowed?

Yes. Pets are allowed with an added pet deposit.

Do I need a special driver's license?

No. A standard Class C driver's license is enough for these rentals, though large vehicle experience is required for the larger rigs.

Can I tow a car or boat?

Yes, up to 5,000 lbs with a tow fee and security deposit.

Can I do a one-way rental?

No. The RV Krew currently does not offer one-way rental options.

Can I travel outside California?

Most rentals are statewide. Trips outside California require written authorization by contract, and special events or festivals require prior approval.
